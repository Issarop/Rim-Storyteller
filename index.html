Simulation notes

The clock runs sixty storyteller checks per in-world day. Randy, Cassandra, Phoebe, and Manual modes are available. Event timing uses a 1.35-day mean time between incidents. Categories use Randy-style weights: Misc 3.5, Major Threat 1.4, Orbital Visitor 1.1, Faction Arrival 2.4, Small Threat 0.6, and Ship Chunk 0.22. After thirteen days without a major threat, the next eligible incident is forced into the Major Threat pool. Threat severity is randomized from 0.5× to 1.5×. Core and DLC incident prompts are writer-facing approximations. The app now includes Royalty, Ideology, Biotech, Anomaly, and Odyssey pools, with DLC toggles, Anomaly activity gating, and planet/orbit/travel context. Exact eligibility and rates still depend on game version, DLC settings, population, wealth, biome, quest state, monolith level, map type, and other hidden game conditions.
